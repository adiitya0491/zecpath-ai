# Aptitude AI Design – Zecpath AI

## Objective

Evaluate candidate logical reasoning, problem-solving ability,
and situational judgment during HR interviews.

---

## Core Components

### 1. Question Engine

Responsible for generating:

* Logical reasoning questions
* Situational judgment scenarios
* Analytical thinking questions

---

### 2. Answer Understanding Engine

Responsible for:

* Understanding candidate responses
* Extracting reasoning patterns
* Identifying decision-making behavior

---

### 3. Aptitude Scoring Engine

Evaluates:

* Logical structure
* Problem-solving clarity
* Decision quality

Produces:

* Aptitude Score (0–100)

---

### 4. Scenario Evaluation Engine

Compares answers against ideal response patterns.

Example:

Team Conflict

Ideal indicators:

* Communicate
* Understand
* Resolve

---

### 5. Output

Produces:

* Aptitude Score
* Detailed Breakdown
* Scenario Evaluation Score
* Strength Areas
* Improvement Areas

---

## Aptitude Scoring Formula

Aptitude Score =

(Structure × 35%)

*

(Problem Solving × 35%)

*

(Decision Making × 30%)

---

## Scenario Weighting

Final Aptitude Score =

(Aptitude Score × 70%)

*

(Scenario Score × 30%)

---

## Ideal Answer Structure

1. Understand Problem

2. Analyze Situation

3. Create Solution

4. Justify Decision

5. Expected Outcome

---

## Advantages

* Measures reasoning ability
* Evaluates decision making
* Works across multiple job roles
* Recruiter-friendly scoring

---

## Limitations

* Rule-based analysis
* Limited contextual understanding
* No deep reasoning validation

---

## Future Improvements

* LLM-based reasoning evaluation
* Multi-step logic verification
* Adaptive cognitive assessments
* Advanced scenario simulations
