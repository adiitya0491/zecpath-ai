# HR Interview Flow Design

## Phase 1 - Introduction

Purpose:
- Build candidate comfort
- Gather background information

Questions:
- Tell me about yourself
- Walk me through your background

---

## Phase 2 - Core HR Questions

Purpose:
- Evaluate communication
- Evaluate personality

Topics:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Teamwork
- Conflict handling

---

## Phase 3 - Role-Based Evaluation

Technical Roles:
- Technical problem solving
- Project discussions
- Learning ability

Non-Technical Roles:
- Client handling
- Leadership
- Business thinking

---

## Phase 4 - Closing

Topics:
- Salary expectations
- Availability
- Relocation

---

## Final Step

Generate HR Evaluation Report
