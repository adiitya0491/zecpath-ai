# Zecpath AI Question & Answer Preparation

## Question 1

How accurate is the AI system?

Answer:

The system achieves approximately 88% to 92% accuracy based on internal testing.

---

## Question 2

How do you reduce bias?

Answer:

We remove sensitive attributes and normalize candidate scores to ensure fair evaluation.

---

## Question 3

Can this scale to large companies?

Answer:

Yes. Zecpath AI uses microservices and horizontal scaling to support large-scale hiring.

---

## Question 4

How is this different from existing ATS systems?

Answer:

Traditional ATS tools only filter resumes. Zecpath AI automates the entire hiring process including interviews, evaluations, and final decisions.

---

## Question 5

Can humans override AI decisions?

Answer:

Yes. Recruiters always have the final authority to accept or reject recommendations.

---

## Question 6

How secure is the system?

Answer:

The platform uses authentication, access control, encryption, audit logs, and governance policies.

---

## Question 7

What are the future plans?

Answer:

Future improvements include AI video interviews, emotion detection, AI coaching, and predictive hiring.
