---

## Step 2: ATS Scoring

The ATS engine analyzes resume-job compatibility.

---

## Step 3: Screening AI

The AI evaluates candidate responses.

---

## Step 4: HR Interview AI

The AI evaluates communication, confidence, and behavioral traits.

---

## Step 5: Technical AI

The AI evaluates technical knowledge.

---

## Step 6: Machine Test

The AI evaluates practical coding skills.

---

## Step 7: Behavior & Integrity Analysis

The system detects behavioral and integrity risks.

---

## Step 8: Decision AI

All scores are combined to generate a final recommendation.

---

## Step 9: Hiring Report

The system generates a recruiter-friendly report.
