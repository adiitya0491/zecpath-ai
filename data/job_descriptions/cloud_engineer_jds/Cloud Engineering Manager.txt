Cloud Engineering Manager
Role Overview
A Cloud Engineering Manager leads cloud engineering teams, balancing technical delivery
with people management and operational excellence.
Key Responsibilities
• Manage cloud engineering teams
• Plan and deliver cloud initiatives
• Support hiring, onboarding, and performance management
• Align technical work with business objectives
• Ensure reliability, security, and cost efficiency
Required Skills
• People management and leadership
• Cloud engineering and architecture
• Agile and project management
• Stakeholder communication
Experience & Qualifications
• 8–14 years of experience, including people management
