Cloud Risk & Controls Engineer

Role Overview
A Cloud Risk & Controls Engineer assesses cloud-related risks and ensures proper controls are implemented to mitigate them.

Key Responsibilities
• Identify and assess cloud security risks
• Design and implement risk mitigation controls
• Conduct risk assessments and audits
• Maintain risk registers and control mappings
• Support regulatory and internal audits

Required Skills
• Risk management frameworks (NIST, ISO, COBIT)
• Cloud security and governance
• Audit and compliance processes
• Strong documentation and reporting skills

Experience & Qualifications
• 5–10 years in risk, controls, or cloud security roles
