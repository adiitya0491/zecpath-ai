Head of Cloud Engineering
Role Overview
The Head of Cloud Engineering owns the overall cloud engineering function, strategy, and
execution across the organization.
Key Responsibilities
• Define cloud engineering vision and roadmap
• Lead multiple cloud teams and managers
• Ensure alignment with business goals
• Oversee budgets, staffing, and performance
• Drive innovation and cloud adoption
Required Skills
• Executive leadership
• Cloud architecture and operations
• Organizational scaling
• Cross-functional collaboration
Experience & Qualifications
• 12–18+ years of IT/cloud experience
