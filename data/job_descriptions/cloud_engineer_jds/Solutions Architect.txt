Solutions Architect (Cloud)
Role Overview
A Cloud Solutions Architect designs cloud solutions that meet specific customer or business
requirements, balancing technical feasibility and cost.
Key Responsibilities
• Gather business and technical requirements
• Design cloud solutions and reference architectures
• Create architecture diagrams and documentation

• Support pre-sales, POCs, and solution validation
• Collaborate with engineering and sales teams
Required Skills
• Cloud solution design
• Application and data architecture
• API and integration patterns
• Cost estimation and optimization
Experience & Qualifications
• 6–10 years of IT experience
• Strong customer-facing and communication skills
