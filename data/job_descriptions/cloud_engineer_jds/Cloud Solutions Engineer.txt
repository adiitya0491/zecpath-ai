Cloud Solutions Engineer
Role Overview
A Cloud Solutions Engineer bridges architecture and implementation, ensuring designed
cloud solutions are successfully deployed and optimized.
Key Responsibilities
• Implement cloud architectures and solutions
• Assist in solution design and validation
• Build and deploy POCs and demos
• Troubleshoot solution-level issues
• Optimize performance, security, and costs
Required Skills
• Hands-on cloud engineering experience
• CICD pipelines and automation
• Containerization and orchestration
• Monitoring and observability
Experience & Qualifications
• 4–8 years of cloud engineering experience
