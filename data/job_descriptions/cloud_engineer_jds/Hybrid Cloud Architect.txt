Hybrid Cloud Architect
Role Overview
A Hybrid Cloud Architect designs and manages architectures that integrate on-premise
infrastructure with public cloud environments.
Key Responsibilities
• Design hybrid cloud architectures
• Integrate on-premise systems with cloud platforms
• Manage connectivity, security, and identity
• Ensure workload portability and resilience
• Optimize hybrid performance and costs
Required Skills
• On-premise infrastructure (VMware, networking)
• Cloud networking and connectivity
• Identity and access management
• Disaster recovery design
Experience & Qualifications

• 7–12 years of infrastructure and cloud experience
