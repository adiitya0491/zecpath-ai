Cloud QA Engineer
Role Overview
A Cloud QA Engineer ensures the quality, reliability, and correctness of cloud-based
applications and infrastructure through structured testing and validation.
Key Responsibilities
• Design and execute test cases for cloud applications
• Validate cloud infrastructure configurations
• Perform functional, integration, and regression testing
• Identify and track defects across cloud environments
• Collaborate with development and DevOps teams
• Ensure quality standards are met before releases
Required Skills
• QA methodologies and testing life cycle
• Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
• Manual and basic automation testing
• Defect tracking tools (Jira, TestRail)
Experience & Qualifications
• 2–5 years of QA or cloud testing experience
