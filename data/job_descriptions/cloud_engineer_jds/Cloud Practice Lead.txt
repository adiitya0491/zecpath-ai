Cloud Practice Lead
Role Overview
A Cloud Practice Lead builds and grows an organization’s cloud practice, including services,
talent, and go-to-market strategy.
Key Responsibilities
• Define cloud practice strategy and offerings
• Build and manage cloud teams and capabilities
• Drive pre-sales and solution design
• Ensure delivery excellence across engagements
• Develop partnerships with cloud vendors
Required Skills
• Cloud architecture and consulting
• Business development and leadership
• Client engagement and pre-sales
• Strategic planning
Experience & Qualifications
• 10–16 years of cloud and consulting experience
