Infrastructure Engineer (Cloud)
Role Overview
A Cloud Infrastructure Engineer designs, builds, and maintains scalable cloud infrastructure
supporting applications and services.
Key Responsibilities
• Provision and manage cloud compute, storage, and networking
• Automate infrastructure deployment and configuration
• Ensure high availability and disaster recovery
• Monitor and optimize infrastructure performance
• Implement security and compliance controls
Required Skills
• Cloud infrastructure services
• Terraform  Ansible
• Linux systems and networking
• Monitoring and logging tools
Experience & Qualifications
• 3–7 years of infrastructure or cloud experience
