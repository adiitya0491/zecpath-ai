Chief Cloud Architect
Role Overview
The Chief Cloud Architect is the highest technical authority for cloud architecture, defining
long-term technical vision and standards.
Key Responsibilities
• Define enterprise cloud architecture vision
• Establish cloud standards and reference architectures
• Guide architecture decisions across teams
• Evaluate emerging technologies
• Partner with executive leadership on strategy
Required Skills
• Deep technical architecture expertise
• Multi-cloud and hybrid architectures
• Security, governance, and compliance
• Strategic thinking and leadership
Experience & Qualifications
• 18–25+ years of IT and architecture experience
