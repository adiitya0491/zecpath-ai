FinOps Engineer
Role Overview
A FinOps Engineer manages cloud financial operations by aligning engineering, finance, and
business teams to optimize cloud spending.
Key Responsibilities
• Monitor and analyze cloud costs
• Implement cost allocation and tagging strategies
• Optimize cloud usage and spend
• Create cost forecasts and budgets
• Enable cost visibility and accountability
• Collaborate with engineering and finance teams
Required Skills
• Cloud cost management tools
• FinOps frameworks and best practices
• Data analysis and reporting
• Cloud architecture fundamentals
Experience & Qualifications
• 3–8 years of cloud or financial operations experience
• FinOps certification preferred
