Autonomous Infrastructure Engineer
Role Overview
An Autonomous Infrastructure Engineer builds self-healing, self-optimizing cloud systems
using AI-driven automation and policy-based management.
Key Responsibilities
• Design autonomous infrastructure systems
• Implement self-healing and auto-remediation
• Integrate AI/ML for operations (AIOps)
• Build policy-driven infrastructure management
• Reduce human intervention in operations
• Improve reliability and efficiency
Required Skills
• AIOps platforms
• Automation & event-driven systems
• Cloud-native & SRE practices
• Policy engines (OPA)
• Observability & monitoring
Experience Level
• 5–10 years
