AI Infrastructure Engineer
Role Overview
An AI Infrastructure Engineer designs and manages cloud infrastructure optimized for
AI/ML workloads, including GPUs, large-scale data pipelines, and model serving platforms.
Key Responsibilities
• Provision GPU/TPU-based infrastructure
• Build scalable ML training and inference platforms
• Optimize performance and cost of AI workloads
• Integrate MLOps pipelines
• Ensure security and data compliance
• Support data scientists and ML engineers
Required Skills
• Cloud AI services & GPU computing
• Kubernetes & model serving
• MLOps tools (Kubeflow, MLflow)
• Data pipelines
• Performance optimization
Experience Level
• 4–9 years
