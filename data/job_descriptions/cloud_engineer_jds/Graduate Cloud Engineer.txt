Graduate Cloud Engineer
Role Overview
A Graduate Cloud Engineer is a fresher role designed for recent graduates to build practical
cloud skills through structured training and real-world projects.
Key Responsibilities
• Assist in cloud infrastructure deployment
• Support system monitoring and operations
• Learn cloud security and best practices
• Work on automation and optimization tasks
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams
Required Skills
• Cloud fundamentals (AWSAzureGCP)
• Basic programming or scripting skills
• Understanding of networking and operating systems
Experience & Qualifications
• Recent graduate (0–1 year experience)
• Degree in Computer Science, IT, or related field
