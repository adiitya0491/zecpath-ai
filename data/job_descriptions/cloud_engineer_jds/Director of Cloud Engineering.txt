Director of Cloud Engineering
Role Overview
A Director of Cloud Engineering provides strategic and operational leadership for large-scale
cloud programs and teams.
Key Responsibilities
• Lead cloud programs and portfolios
• Define KPIs and success metrics
• Manage senior leaders and managers
• Partner with product, security, and finance teams
• Ensure cloud governance and compliance
Required Skills
• Program and portfolio management
• Cloud strategy and governance
• Leadership at scale
• Financial and operational oversight
Experience & Qualifications
• 14–20+ years of experience
