Cloud Governance Engineer

Role Overview
A Cloud Governance Engineer establishes and enforces policies for cloud usage, cost, security, and resource management.

Key Responsibilities
• Define cloud governance frameworks
• Implement policies for access, cost, and usage
• Enforce tagging, naming, and resource standards
• Monitor compliance and policy violations
• Collaborate with FinOps and security teams

Required Skills
• Cloud policy management tools
• Cost management and FinOps concepts
• IAM and access governance
• Automation and reporting

Experience & Qualifications
• 4–9 years of cloud governance experience
