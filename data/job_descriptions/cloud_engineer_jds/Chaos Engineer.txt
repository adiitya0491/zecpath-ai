Chaos Engineer
Role Overview
A Chaos Engineer proactively tests system resilience by injecting failures into cloud systems
to identify weaknesses before they impact users.
Key Responsibilities
• Design and execute chaos experiments
• Test system resilience under failure conditions
• Improve fault tolerance and recovery strategies
• Collaborate with SRE and platform teams
• Document findings and resilience improvements
Required Skills
• Chaos engineering tools (Chaos Monkey, Litmus, Gremlin)
• Distributed systems and cloud architectures
• Observability and monitoring
• Strong automation skills
Experience & Qualifications
• 5–10 years of cloud, SRE, or platform engineering experience
