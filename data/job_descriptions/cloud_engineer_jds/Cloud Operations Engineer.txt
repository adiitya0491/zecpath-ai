Cloud Operations Engineer
Role Overview
The Cloud Operations Engineer ensures day-to-day stability, performance, and availability of
cloud environments.
Key Responsibilities
• Monitor cloud infrastructure and services
• Respond to incidents and outages
• Perform routine maintenance and patching
• Manage alerts and operational dashboards
• Support release and deployment activities
Required Skills
• Incident management
• Monitoring tools (Datadog, CloudWatch, New Relic)
• ITIL / operational best practices
• Scripting for automation
Experience & Qualifications
• 2–5 years in cloud operations or IT operations
