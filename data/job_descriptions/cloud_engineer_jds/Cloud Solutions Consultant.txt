Cloud Solutions Consultant
Role Overview
A Cloud Solutions Consultant works closely with sales, pre-sales, and delivery teams to
design cloud solutions tailored to customer needs.
Key Responsibilities
• Understand customer requirements and use cases
• Design cloud-based solutions and proposals
• Create architecture diagrams and cost estimates
• Support proof-of-concepts (PoCs)
• Assist in cloud product demonstrations
• Bridge business and technical teams
Required Skills
• Solution architecture
• Customer-facing communication
• Cloud pricing & service models
• Pre-sales & technical documentation
• Multi-cloud exposure
Experience Level
• 4–7 years
