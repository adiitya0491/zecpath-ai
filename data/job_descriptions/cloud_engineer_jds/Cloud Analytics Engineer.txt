Cloud Analytics Engineer
Role Overview
A Cloud Analytics Engineer designs and maintains analytics platforms enabling business
intelligence and data-driven decision-making.
Key Responsibilities
• Build analytics data models and dashboards
• Optimize query performance and costs
• Manage BI tools and data access
• Ensure data accuracy and governance
• Support business analytics teams
Required Skills
• Cloud analytics services
• SQL and data modeling
• BI tools (Power BI, Looker, Tableau)
• Data governance principles
Experience & Qualifications
• 3–6 years of analytics or data engineering experience
