Cloud Backup & DR Engineer
Role Overview
A Cloud Backup & Disaster Recovery Engineer ensures data protection, business continuity,
and rapid recovery from failures or disasters.
Key Responsibilities
• Design and implement backup and DR strategies
• Configure backup policies and replication
• Test disaster recovery plans regularly
• Ensure compliance with RPO and RTO requirements
• Automate backup and recovery processes
Required Skills
• Cloud backup and DR services
• High availability and replication strategies
• Automation and scripting
• Compliance and audit readiness
Experience & Qualifications
• 4–9 years of backup, DR, or cloud infrastructure experience
