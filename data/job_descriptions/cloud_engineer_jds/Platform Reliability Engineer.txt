Platform Reliability Engineer
Role Overview
A Platform Reliability Engineer ensures high availability, scalability, and performance of
internal cloud platforms using reliability engineering principles.
Key Responsibilities
• Design resilient platform architectures
• Monitor platform health and performance
• Automate recovery and scaling processes

• Conduct reliability testing and chaos engineering
• Collaborate with platform and SRE teams
Required Skills
• Reliability engineering and SRE concepts
• Kubernetes and platform systems
• Automation and scripting
• Observability tools
Experience & Qualifications
• 5–9 years of platform or reliability engineering experience
