Hybrid Infrastructure Engineer
Role Overview
A Hybrid Infrastructure Engineer manages and integrates on-premise infrastructure with
cloud platforms to deliver seamless hybrid environments.
Key Responsibilities
• Design and maintain hybrid infrastructure solutions
• Integrate on-premise systems with cloud services
• Manage compute, storage, and networking across environments
• Ensure security, compliance, and performance
• Support hybrid migration and modernization projects
Required Skills
• On-premise infrastructure (VMware, SAN, networking)
• Cloud platforms and hybrid services
• Identity and access management
• Disaster recovery and backup strategies
Experience & Qualifications
• 5–10 years of infrastructure and cloud experience
