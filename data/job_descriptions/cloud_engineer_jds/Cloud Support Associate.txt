Cloud Support Associate
Role Overview
A Cloud Support Associate provides first-level support for cloud platforms, helping users
troubleshoot issues and maintain service availability.
Key Responsibilities
• Respond to cloud-related support tickets
• Perform basic troubleshooting and issue resolution
• Monitor cloud resources and alerts
• Escalate complex issues to senior engineers
• Maintain support documentation and knowledge base
Required Skills
• Cloud fundamentals
• Troubleshooting and communication skills
• LinuxWindows and networking basics
• Ticketing tools (ServiceNow, Jira – preferred)
Experience & Qualifications
• 0–2 years of IT support or cloud support experience
• Relevant cloud certification is a plus
