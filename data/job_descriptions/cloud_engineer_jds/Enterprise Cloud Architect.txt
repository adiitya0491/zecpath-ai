Enterprise Cloud Architect
Role Overview

An Enterprise Cloud Architect defines cloud strategy and architecture at an organizational
level, ensuring alignment with enterprise standards and long-term goals.
Key Responsibilities
• Define enterprise-wide cloud architecture strategy
• Establish cloud governance, security, and compliance
• Design large-scale, multi-team cloud platforms
• Align cloud initiatives with business strategy
• Guide and mentor cloud architects and engineers
Required Skills
• Enterprise architecture frameworks (TOGAF)
• Cloud governance and compliance
• Hybrid and large-scale cloud systems
• Stakeholder management
Experience & Qualifications
• 10–15+ years of IT experience
• Strong enterprise architecture background
