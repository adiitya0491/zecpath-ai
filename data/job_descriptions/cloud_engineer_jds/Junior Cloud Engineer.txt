Junior Cloud Engineer
Role Overview
A Junior Cloud Engineer assists in deploying, managing, and maintaining cloud
infrastructure while learning cloud best practices under the guidance of senior engineers.
Key Responsibilities
• Assist in deploying and configuring cloud resources
• Monitor cloud systems and respond to basic alerts
• Support automation and infrastructure scripts
• Perform basic troubleshooting of cloud services
• Maintain documentation and operational guides
Required Skills
• Basic knowledge of AWS  Azure  GCP
• Understanding of cloud compute, storage, and networking
• LinuxWindows fundamentals
• Basic scripting (Bash  Python – preferred)
Experience & Qualifications

• Bachelor’s degree in IT, CS, or related field
• 0–2 years of experience or relevant cloud training
