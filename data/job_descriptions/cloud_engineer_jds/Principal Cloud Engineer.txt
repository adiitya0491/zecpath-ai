Principal Cloud Engineer
Role Overview
A Principal Cloud Engineer is a top-level technical authority who drives innovation, platform
strategy, and complex cloud system design.
Key Responsibilities
• Design enterprise-scale cloud architectures
• Solve complex technical challenges
• Define cloud engineering standards
• Lead strategic technical initiatives
• Mentor senior engineers and architects
• Evaluate emerging cloud technologies
Required Skills
• Deep cloud platform expertise
• Distributed systems design
• Cloud security and governance
• Strategic technical leadership
Experience & Qualifications
• 10–15 years of IT experience, strong cloud background
