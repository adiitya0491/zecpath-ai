Cloud Platform Engineer
Role Overview
A Cloud Platform Engineer builds and maintains internal cloud platforms that enable teams
to deploy and operate applications efficiently.
Key Responsibilities
• Develop internal developer platforms (IDP)
• Manage Kubernetes and container platforms
• Build self-service infrastructure tools
• Improve developer experience and platform reliability
• Ensure security, scalability, and observability
Required Skills
• Kubernetes, Docker, Helm
• Cloud-native architectures

• CICD and automation
• Observability and monitoring tools
Experience & Qualifications
• 4–8 years of cloud or platform engineering experience
