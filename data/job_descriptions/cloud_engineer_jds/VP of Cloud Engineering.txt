VP of Cloud Engineering
Role Overview
The VP of Cloud Engineering sets enterprise cloud strategy, drives transformation, and
ensures cloud capabilities support long-term business growth.
Key Responsibilities
• Define and execute cloud strategy
• Lead large, global cloud organizations
• Drive cloud innovation and modernization
• Manage budgets, vendors, and partnerships
• Report progress to executive leadership
Required Skills
• Executive leadership and vision
• Cloud platforms and enterprise architecture
• Business and financial acumen
• Change management
Experience & Qualifications
• 15–22+ years of IT/cloud leadership experience
