Cloud Support Engineer
Role Overview
A Cloud Support Engineer provides technical support for cloud services and assists internal
or external customers in resolving cloud-related issues.
Key Responsibilities
• Diagnose and resolve cloud service issues
• Respond to tickets and customer queries
• Provide technical guidance on cloud usage
• Escalate complex issues to engineering teams
• Document solutions and best practices
Required Skills
• Cloud fundamentals
• Troubleshooting and customer support skills
• Networking and OS basics
• Ticketing systems (ServiceNow, Jira)
Experience & Qualifications
• 0–3 years of cloud or IT support experience
