Cloud Reliability Engineer
Role Overview
The Cloud Reliability Engineer ensures high availability, resilience, and reliability of cloud
systems using SRE principles.
Key Responsibilities
• Design fault-tolerant cloud systems
• Define SLAs, SLOs, and error budgets
• Automate reliability and recovery processes

• Conduct root cause analysis
• Improve system observability
Required Skills
• SRE practices
• Automation and scripting
• Monitoring, logging, and tracing
• Distributed systems knowledge
Experience & Qualifications
• 4–8 years of cloud or SRE experience
