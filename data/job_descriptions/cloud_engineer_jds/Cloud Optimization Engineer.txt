Cloud Optimization Engineer
Role Overview
A Cloud Optimization Engineer continuously improves cloud environments for cost
efficiency, performance, and operational excellence.
Key Responsibilities
• Analyze cloud usage and identify optimization opportunities
• Optimize compute, storage, and network resources
• Implement autoscaling and rightsizing strategies
• Improve reliability and efficiency
• Collaborate with FinOps and operations teams
Required Skills
• Cloud monitoring and cost tools
• Performance tuning and capacity planning
• Automation and scripting
• FinOps fundamentals
Experience & Qualifications
• 3–7 years of cloud engineering experience
