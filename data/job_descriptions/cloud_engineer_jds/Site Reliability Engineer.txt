Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)
Role Overview
A Site Reliability Engineer applies software engineering practices to ensure reliability,
scalability, and performance of cloud-based systems.
Key Responsibilities
• Define and manage SLAs, SLOs, and error budgets
• Automate operational tasks and incident response
• Improve system reliability and fault tolerance
• Monitor systems using observability tools
• Perform root cause analysis and post-incident reviews
Required Skills
• SRE principles and practices
• Cloud infrastructure and distributed systems
• Monitoring, logging, and tracing tools
• Strong automation and coding skills
Experience & Qualifications
• 4–8 years of experience in cloud or SRE roles
