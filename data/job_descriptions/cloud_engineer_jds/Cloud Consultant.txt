Cloud Consultant
Role Overview
A Cloud Consultant advises organizations on cloud strategy, architecture, migration, and best
practices, aligning technology with business goals.
Key Responsibilities
• Assess current IT infrastructure and readiness
• Define cloud adoption and migration strategies
• Recommend cloud services, architectures, and tools
• Guide security, compliance, and governance models
• Support implementation and vendor selection
• Conduct workshops and stakeholder presentations
Required Skills
• Cloud architecture & design
• Business and technical communication
• Security & compliance (ISO, SOC, GDPR, HIPAA)
• Cost optimization & FinOps basics
• Multi-cloud knowledge
Experience Level
• 5+ years in cloud or IT consulting
