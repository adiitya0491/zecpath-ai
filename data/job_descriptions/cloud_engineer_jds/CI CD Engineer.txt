CI/CD Engineer (Cloud)
Role Overview
A CICD Engineer designs and manages automated build, test, and deployment pipelines in
cloud environments.
Key Responsibilities
• Design and maintain CICD pipelines
• Integrate testing, security, and quality checks
• Optimize build and deployment performance
• Support release management and automation
• Maintain pipeline documentation and standards
Required Skills
• CICD tools (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI)
• Cloud deployment strategies
• Scripting and automation
• Container-based deployments
Experience & Qualifications
• 3–6 years of CICD or DevOps experience
