Managed Services Cloud Engineer
Role Overview
A Managed Services Cloud Engineer operates and supports cloud environments on behalf of
clients, ensuring uptime, security, and performance.
Key Responsibilities
• Monitor and manage cloud infrastructure 24/7
• Handle incidents, outages, and escalations
• Perform patching, upgrades, and backups
• Ensure SLA, SLO, and compliance adherence
• Implement security best practices
• Provide regular operational reports
Required Skills
• Cloud operations & monitoring
• ITIL & incident management
• Backup, DR, and high availability
• Security & access management
• Automation and scripting
Experience Level
• 2–5 years
