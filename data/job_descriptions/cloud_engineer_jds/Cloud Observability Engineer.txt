Cloud Observability Engineer
Role Overview
A Cloud Observability Engineer builds advanced monitoring, logging, and tracing systems to
provide deep visibility into cloud-native and distributed systems.
Key Responsibilities
• Design observability architectures
• Implement metrics, logs, and traces
• Optimize alerting and incident detection
• Support SRE and DevOps teams
• Improve system transparency and insights
• Enable proactive incident prevention
Required Skills
• Observability tools (Prometheus, OpenTelemetry)
• Distributed systems knowledge
• SRE practices
• Data analysis & visualization
• Cloud platforms
Experience Level
• 3–8 years
