Lead Cloud Engineer
Role Overview
A Lead Cloud Engineer provides technical leadership for cloud initiatives, ensuring
successful delivery and alignment with architectural standards.
Key Responsibilities
• Lead cloud engineering teams and projects
• Define technical standards and best practices
• Review and approve cloud architectures
• Coordinate with DevOps, security, and application teams
• Support planning and delivery timelines
Required Skills
• Cloud architecture and engineering
• Team leadership and mentoring
• CI/CD and automation
• Strong communication skills
Experience & Qualifications
• 7–12 years of IT/cloud experience
