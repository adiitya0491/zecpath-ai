Kubernetes Platform Engineer
Role Overview
A Kubernetes Platform Engineer builds and operates scalable Kubernetes platforms that serve
multiple development teams reliably and securely.
Key Responsibilities
• Design and manage Kubernetes clusters
• Build internal developer platforms (IDPs)
• Implement security, RBAC, and network policies
• Automate cluster provisioning and upgrades
• Improve reliability and performance of platforms
• Support developers using Kubernetes services
Required Skills
• Advanced Kubernetes (EKS, AKS, GKE)
• Helm, Operators, and CRDs
• Platform engineering concepts
• Observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana)
• Networking & security
Experience Level
• 4–8 years
