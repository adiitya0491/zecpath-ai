Cloud Cost Optimization Analyst
Role Overview
A Cloud Cost Optimization Analyst focuses on analyzing cloud spend and providing
actionable insights to reduce waste and improve financial efficiency.
Key Responsibilities
• Analyze cloud billing and usage data
• Identify underutilized and idle resources
• Recommend cost-saving strategies
• Track savings and ROI
• Create dashboards and reports for stakeholders
Required Skills
• Cloud billing and cost analysis tools
• Strong analytical and reporting skills
• SQL, Excel, or BI tools
• FinOps concepts
Experience & Qualifications
• 2–6 years of cloud cost analysis or IT finance experience
